About

I started caring about access before I started writing code. Volunteering at an orphanage school in India, I watched bright kids lose out for one reason: the tools around them weren't built with them in mind. That hasn't stopped bothering me.

Now I study Financial Technology at NJIT with a concurrent Tech MBA concentrated in Artificial Intelligence. I sit between business and engineering on purpose. Strategy without working software is a slide deck. Software without strategy doesn't ship.

My work centers on people most products skip: neurodivergent students, underserved communities. AdaptIQ came from that. So did my accessibility research. The long-term goal is intelligent systems that are accessible, efficient, and secure for the people who need them most. We're not there. I'm building toward it.
